# VTS Fuel Sender Instructions

Source: Vintage Tank Solutions (VTS) — [`docs/vts-fuel-sender-instructions.jpeg`](../docs/vts-fuel-sender-instructions.jpeg)

> The factory Datsun wiring colors referenced below may not be 100% accurate for all models. Always test the factory leads with a digital multimeter to verify their actual identification prior to connecting battery power to test the fuel sender.

## Wiring Reference

**Note:** This wiring is for the factory dash fuel gauge / chassis harness, **not** the AEM Infinity engine wiring harness. Included here for reference since it's good to know when integrating the fuel sender with the rest of the car's wiring.

| On Fuel Sender | 1970–1974.5 240Z/260Z Factory Wire Harness | 1974.5–1978 260Z/280Z Factory Wire Harness |
|---|---|---|
| **BLACK** | = GROUND (Black wire) | = GROUND (Black wire) |
| **PINK** | = FUEL GAUGE SIGNAL (Yellow wire) | = FUEL GAUGE SIGNAL (Yellow wire) |
| **YELLOW** | = N/A (Optional low level fuel lamp) | = LOW LEVEL FUEL LAMP (Yellow wire w/Blue Stripe) |

## To Install

1. Plan out the locations of the fuel sender and fuel pump module on the poly tank prior to drilling any holes so that the fuel hoses and electrical wiring route to their respective systems. These components can be installed next to each other, but ensure they will not make contact with one another. Some fuel pump modules use fuel filter baffle trays and/or long fuel filters which can interfere with other components, so plan accordingly. We recommend drilling the fuel pump module hole first so that it'll aid in the installation of the fuel sender. If the fuel pump hole is large enough to place your hand through, this will make installation easier.
2. Use the included nitrile gasket as a template to trace the one large fuel sender hole (**1.50 inch diameter**) and five hardware holes on the flat mounting pad location of the poly tank. We suggest using masking tape on the tank to provide a visible area to trace the marks with a black pen. **NOTE:** the nitrile gasket, steel C-ring, and fuel sender head are all directional. Meaning that all three components must be installed so that the five hardware holes perfectly align. The small tab found on the side of the gasket aligns with the gap in the steel C-ring, which also aligns with the exit direction for the 3-wires coming off the sender head. Direct the sender wires to the location of the factory wire harness to minimize the harness length.
3. Once all holes are traced, drill them out, and clean up any plastic flashing. A 1.50 inch drill saw can be used for the main sender hole. We suggest making the five (5) pan head Phillips 10-32 threaded screw holes through the poly tank slightly larger than the hardware, just enough so that they easily slide through the tank wall without getting hung up.
4. Using the larger fuel pump drilled hole, which you should have previously drilled out on the poly tank mounting pad, slip your hand through this hole with the steel C-ring and slide the sender (with gasket under the head) through the steel C-ring. *If you cannot slip your hand through the fuel pump hanger hole, visit our website page: Install Videos / Customer Feedback, for a tutorial video.*
5. Using one 10-32 pan head screw, start to align all three components by hand. **NOTE: The large gap in the steel C-ring will only align with the tab on the gasket and exit direction of the wiring off the sender head for all three components to be correctly installed.**
6. With a second pan head screw, continue to align the components until the C-ring is hanging with two screws threaded through it.
7. Push the C-ring up to the tank mounting pad wall and hold it there while continuing to place the remaining pan head screws through the sender head/gasket. Using a handheld screwdriver, thread all the screws through the C-ring so that you no longer have to hold it up to the internal mounting pad wall. Finish securing the screws using a star pattern to 15 lb-in (inch pounds). *We highly suggest performing this all by hand so that you can feel each screw thread into the C-ring. They should not bind or be difficult to thread. If so, check that you have aligned all three components as described above.*
   **WARNING: DO NOT USE ANY POWER TOOLS and DO NOT OVERTIGHTEN.**
